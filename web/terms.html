Project and operator

Depth Atlas is an independent project by Team Seifuku. Gaurav Ranade created the website and operates it from Pune, Maharashtra, India. Adwita Kurle is the team leader. See About Us for the team.

This is a development prototype. It is not an official INCOIS advisory service. The problem statement and source citations do not imply institutional approval, deployment or scientific endorsement.

Using the app

You may explore the public prototype for learning, research exploration, demonstration and evaluation. Use it lawfully and respect the published resource limits. Do not disrupt or overload the service, bypass access controls, exploit vulnerabilities or present its results as official advisories.

Only import files you are authorized to send to the service. Do not upload confidential or personal information. Supported observation files are parsed temporarily; unsupported schemas and oversized files are rejected. An accepted import is not independent scientific validation. Imported investigations embed the original file in local records and JSON/ZIP and offline HTML downloads. Only share these copies where you have permission under the original data terms; checksums do not establish ownership or authenticity.

Read results with their limits

The app provides historical model fields, original instrument profiles, derived analyses and simulations. It does not show live ocean conditions. Read each result with its source, date, coordinates, units, quality flags and method limitations. Display colours and exaggerated depth help make data visible; they are not the ocean's natural appearance.

Models and measurements
Model output is not a direct measurement. Agreement with observations that may have been assimilated does not establish independent validation. Imported temperature and salinity can be compared after explicit matching checks. Observed chlorophyll has no matching model field in this release. Observation support describes this collection and these rules, not calibrated confidence.
Drift and virtual expeditions
Particles are fixed-depth passive tracers in historical currents, not sinking pebbles or measured drifters. Simulated stations, routes and reconstruction experiments do not establish safe navigation, forecast improvement or operational observing-system impact. Arrival fractions are not real-world probabilities.
Heat and climate
A surface event does not prove unusual heat throughout the water column. Climate comparisons cover selected historical seasons against a declared baseline; they do not establish causation or predict future events.
Evolution and blackout
Overlapping shapes do not prove that the same water travelled between locations. Excluding observations changes the displayed comparison evidence, not the underlying model or its assimilation.
Wider source regions
Coverage depends on the packaged source, dates and coordinates. It does not make every laboratory available everywhere. GOBAI-O2 is an external machine-learning reconstruction with separate provider uncertainty, not a model trained by this team or a direct observation. Its pressure coordinate remains in decibars.

Do not use the prototype for navigation, search and rescue, hazard warnings, fisheries operations or other safety-critical decisions. Use the responsible official services for operational information.

Saves, downloads and sharing

Saved investigations and regional packs are local browser copies, not cloud backups. Download a portable copy if you need to keep it or move it to another device. Browser cleanup can remove local records. Imported replay reparses the embedded original file with the recorded parser and column mapping. Model data remains a separate pinned dependency. Replay depends on the source and method versions remaining available; a mismatch prevents reopening with silently substituted results.

Offline viewers display stored snapshots, not updated conditions or freshly recalculated results. They support observation profiles, model comparisons and saved native model columns. Full model grids and other laboratory computations require the online service. Deleting a browser record does not delete downloaded copies.

Checksums and matching replay establish file consistency or reproducibility, not author identity or independent scientific accuracy. Shared links reveal their titles and settings to recipients. Keep source attribution, historical dates, quality flags and limitations with any shared report or numerical subset.

When sharing a view, credit Depth Atlas by Team Seifuku as the interface and retain the original dataset and method references. Do not imply that the team, INCOIS or data providers endorse your interpretation.

Learning and explanation tools

Basic and In-depth tutorials demonstrate supported functions over prepared historical cases. The explanation panel reads current structured results and reviewed definitions. Suggested queries are shown before being applied. These aids do not certify a result, provide missing measurements, answer arbitrary questions or predict hazards. No external language-model service is connected.
